title: Clinical Research
order: 1
---
Our clinical research focuses on pain mechanisms and neurological recovery in people with spinal cord injury (SCI), stroke, traumatic brain injury (TBI), and cerebral palsy. We use clinical neurophysiology, motor unit number estimation, and clinical data science to characterize recovery trajectories and identify clinically relevant predictors and confounders. We also conduct clinical trials targeting SCI-related neuropathic pain and post-traumatic headache following mild TBI.
