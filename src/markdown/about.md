The Pain & Neurorecovery Group is part of the University Clinic for Neurorehabilitation at Aarhus University and is based at Hammel Neurocenter, one of Denmark’s two highly specialized national neurorehabilitation centers for people with acquired brain injury. 
The group brings together experimental, translational, and clinical research. Our work encompasses psychophysics, computational modelling, clinical neurophysiology, clinical trials, and clinical data science. We also operate the Hammel Pain Physiology Lab, an embedded hospital-based laboratory that provides patients with convenient, barrier-free access to experimental pain studies and clinical trials.
