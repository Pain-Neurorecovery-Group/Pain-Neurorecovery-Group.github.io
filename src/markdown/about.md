The Pain & Neurorecovery Group is part of the University Clinic for Neurorehabilitation at Aarhus University and is based at Hammel Neurocenter, a highly specialized national center for neurorehabilitation after acquired brain injury. 
The group brings together experimental, translational, and clinical expertise across multiple disciplines. It also operates the **Hammel Pain Physiology Lab**, an embedded hospital-based facility that gives participants convenient access to experimental studies and clinical trials.
